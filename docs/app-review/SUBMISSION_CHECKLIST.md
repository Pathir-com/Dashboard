## Submit one permission at a time

Submitting permissions individually rather than as a bundle gives Meta less surface area to reject the whole package. If one permission gets approved and another doesn't, the approved one ships immediately.

## After submission

- Watch `mintdental2025@gmail.com` daily — Meta sends follow-ups by email
- If Meta requests additional info or a revised screencast, respond within 7 days or the submission auto-rejects
- Standard turnaround: 3-7 business days

## After approval

- Permissions move from "Standard Access" to "Advanced Access" automatically
- The "Connect with Facebook" / "Connect with Instagram" buttons in the Pathir dashboard now work for any FB/IG account on the planet — no Tester invite required
- You can remove old testers from App Roles if you want, but they continue to work
